# ScamShield UI Copy & Microcopy Guidelines

This document provides official microcopy, accessibility wording, and plain-language definitions for the ScamShield frontend interface.

---

## 1. App Tagline
**ScamShield AI: Simple, instant protection against online payment frauds, fake messages, and suspicious links.**

---

## 2. Trust Line
> **We never ask for your OTP, PIN, passwords, or bank account details.**

---

## 3. Empty-State Messages

1. **Text & Message Scanner**:
   *“Paste any suspicious SMS, WhatsApp message, or email here to check if it is safe.”*

2. **Website & Link Scanner**:
   *“Enter a website link here to check if it leads to a fake login or malicious phishing page.”*

3. **Screenshot & Image Scanner**:
   *“Upload a screenshot of any message or payment request to extract and analyze the text.”*

4. **Safety Copilot Assistant**:
   *“Ask any question about digital payments, cyber safety, or suspicious messages in simple words.”*

---

## 4. Verdict Labels & Plain-Language Meanings
Designed for high readability and clarity for all users, including elderly and non-technical citizens:

- **SAFE**:
  *“This message or website looks normal, but remember to never share your PIN or OTP with anyone.”*

- **SUSPICIOUS**:
  *“Warning: This looks like a fraud attempt designed to take your money or personal details — do not respond.”*

- **KNOWN**:
  *“Danger: This link is confirmed on fraud lists as a dangerous fake website — do not open it under any circumstance.”*

- **INSUFFICIENT**:
  *“We could not find enough information to be completely sure — please verify directly with your bank branch.”*

---

## 5. User Consent Notice (2 Sentences)
By checking a message, link, or image, you consent to our automated system analyzing its content strictly for cyber threat and scam detection. We do not store personal contact numbers, bank passwords, or private identity records on our servers.

---

## 6. Limitations Note (2 Sentences)
ScamShield AI provides automated guidance to help protect you, but no automated security system can be 100% infallible. If you are uncertain or suspect you have lost money, always contact your bank immediately or dial the 1930 national cyber helpline.
