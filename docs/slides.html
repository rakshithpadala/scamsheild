Slide 01 / 14 • Project Identity

ScamShield AI: Multimodal Digital-Safety Assistant

  • Focus & Context: India-tailored multi-modal defense engine targeting UPI fraud, APK drops, and digital arrest extortion.
  • Core Philosophy: Detect → Investigate → Explain → Verify → Act Safely.
  • Research Contribution: Evidence-based explainability combining statistical NLP, deterministic rules, and cited regulatory RAG.
Slide 02 / 14 • Problem Statement

The Escalating Indian Cyber Threat Landscape

  • High-Velocity Deception: Over 13 billion monthly UPI transactions targeted by deceptive collect requests disguised as cashbacks.
  • Psychological Extortion: Forged law enforcement notices asserting fake "Digital Arrest" over WhatsApp and video calls.
  • Opaque Existing Tools: Standard filters flag binary "spam" without explaining fraud mechanics or citing official relief channels.
Slide 03 / 14 • Objectives & Principles

System Objectives & Non-Negotiable Principles

  • Multi-Modal Ingestion: Unified evaluation across SMS text, device screenshots (OCR), raw links, and forwarded emails.
  • Deterministic Safety: The LLM never decides the verdict; decisions derive from calibrated ML models and threat feeds.
  • Recall Prioritization: Optimized for high Recall (93.96%) to minimize dangerous false negatives (missed scams).
Slide 04 / 14 • Architecture

End-to-End System Architecture

  • Extraction Layer: Bilateral filtered Tesseract OCR paired with regex entity extractors (UPI IDs, URLs, phone numbers).
  • Parallel Inference Engines: Binary statistical NLP model, 10-class scam category classifier, 12+ expert heuristic rules, and OpenPhish/URLhaus feed matchers.
  • Calibrated Output: 0–100 risk score, 4 discrete verdicts, highlighted suspicious text spans, and cited NPCI/RBI advisories.
Slide 05 / 14 • Data Engineering

Datasets & Research Honesty Statement

  • Foundational Baselines: UCI SMS Spam (5,574 rows), Tranco Top-1M benign domains, and active OpenPhish/URLhaus feeds.
  • Calibrated Synthetic Corpus: 1,000 synthetic Indian scam samples across 10 categories generated via randomized slot templates.
  • Independent Test Benchmark: 60-row hand-crafted gold test set (40 scams + 20 benign hard negatives) with 0 overlap / zero leakage.
Slide 06 / 14 • Text Model Benchmarks

Binary Text Scam Detection (UCI SMS Benchmark)

  • Benchmarked 6 Classifiers: Evaluated Logistic Regression, Linear SVM, Random Forest, XGBoost, Decision Tree, and KNN.
  • Selected Model: Logistic Regression achieved 93.96% Recall, 0.0093 FPR, and 0.9920 ROC-AUC.
  • Safety Decision Rationale: Prioritizing recall guarantees minimal missed scams (FNR: 6.04%) with sub-millisecond inference.
Slide 07 / 14 • Category Classification

10-Class Scam Category Model Results

  • 10 Indian Threat Classes: Bank KYC, UPI Collect, Cashback/Lottery, Job, Investment, Loan, Delivery, Police, Tech Support, Other.
  • Representation Comparison: Sparse TF-IDF n-grams achieved 82.50% Accuracy & 0.8124 Macro F1 vs MiniLM embeddings (80.00%).
  • Deployment Efficiency: Selected TF-IDF + Logistic Regression operates with <1MB memory footprint and zero cold-start latency.
Slide 08 / 14 • Heuristics & Threat Intel

Expert Rule Engine & Threat Intelligence Feeds

  • 12+ Heuristic Rules: Unit-tested triggers for urgency extortion, UPI PIN entry demands, and digital arrest threats.
  • Span-Level Explainability: Locates exact start/end character offsets for suspicious phrases, highlighted directly in the UI.
  • Live Threat Intel Matching: Verified OpenPhish / URLhaus feed matches trigger an immediate KNOWN_THREAT override.
Slide 09 / 14 • Risk Fusion

Calibrated Multi-Modal Risk Fusion Engine

  • Transparent Linear Weights: Fusion score = 0.45 × Rules + 0.35 × Statistical ML + 0.20 × Lexical URL risk.
  • Four Discrete Verdicts: KNOWN_THREAT (95–100), SUSPICIOUS (60–94), LOW_RISK (0–30), and INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE.
  • SSRF-Safe Design: Submitted URLs are lexically analyzed without server-side HTTP rendering to eliminate server vulnerabilities.
Slide 10 / 14 • RAG & Assistance

Retrieval-Augmented Guidance (RAG) & 1930 Relief

  • Official Corpus: Indexed 19 regulatory advisories from Reserve Bank of India (RBI), NPCI, and Indian Cyber Crime Coordination Centre (I4C).
  • Core Banking Truths: Enforces critical public awareness guidance (e.g., "A UPI PIN is never required to receive money").
  • Emergency Protocol: Automatically routes users to the National Cyber Crime Helpline (1930) and cybercrime.gov.in.

Slide 13 / 14 • Ethics & Safety

Ethics, Credential Guardrails & Limitations

  • Zero Credential Ingestion: System never solicits, stores, or logs UPI PINs, passwords, CVVs, or OTPs.
  • Responsible AI Claims: Generates "high-risk characteristics detected", never "definitely a scam", with transparent limitations notices.
  • Free-Tier & Offline Resilience: Deterministic template fallbacks guarantee continuous operation during network or quota exhaustion.
